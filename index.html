About Me

Get to know me!

Hey! It's Brandon Schuitema and I really like making games. I love working on programming and 3D modelling the most when creating a game. My favourite types of projects to work on are fun, creative, and unique games. I like to have fun with what I'm creating. I like to put my all into what I create, so why not have the experience be enjoyable.

Below are a few of my academic achievements:

-Game Design diploma from Conestoga College with High Distinction
-Passed first round of Ubisoft NEXT 2024/ 2025 for Level Design
-Graduated High School with an Honour Roll for grades 9, 10, 11, and 12
-Earned a Creative Writing Award in High School
